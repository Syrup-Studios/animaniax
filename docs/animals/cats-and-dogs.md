# Cats and Dogs

Animaniax adds named cat and dog breeds with care timers, pet beds, hunting rules, and herding behavior.

## Cats

### Breeds and where to find them

| Breed | Coat appearance | Where to obtain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ocelot** | Red | Spawns naturally in Jungles and Savannas |
| **American Shorthair** | British Shorthair | Pet Seller villager (Novice) |
| **Asiatic** | Calico | Pet Seller villager (Apprentice) |
| **Ragdoll** | Ragdoll | Pet Seller villager (Apprentice) |
| **Tabby** | Tabby | Pet Seller villager (Apprentice) |
| **Exotic** | Persian | Pet Seller villager (Journeyman) |
| **Norwegian** | White | Pet Seller villager (Journeyman) |
| **Siamese** | Siamese | Pet Seller villager (Journeyman) |

All breeds are also available from Creative spawn eggs. By default, Animaniax cats replace natural vanilla ocelot spawns in Jungles and Savannas.

### Stats, care, and behavior

- **Health and attack:** 9 hearts of health, 1.25 hearts of attack damage.
- **Taming and feeding:** Feed Raw Fish (Cod, Salmon, Tropical Fish, or Pufferfish) by hand or in a **Pet Bowl** to tame, feed, and breed cats. Give them water with a Water Bucket or Pet Bowl.
- **Hunting:** Wild (untamed) cats that are awake and standing hunt frogs, toads, baby chicks, baby peachicks, hamsters, ferrets, hedgehogs, and silverfish.
- **Sleep:** Cats sleep at **night** (sunset to dawn) on a **Cat Bed**.
- **Breeding:** Two Content, fertile, opposite-sex adult cats can breed. Kittens inherit a parent's breed and keep the owner if both parents share the same owner.

## Dogs

### Breeds and where to find them

Animaniax adds 15 dog breeds:

- **Wild breeds:** **Wolf** spawns in Forests, Mountains, and snowy biomes; **Fox** has a 50% chance to spawn in Forests and snowy biomes. By default, Animaniax dogs replace natural vanilla wolf spawns.
- **Pet Seller breeds** (see [Villages and World Generation](../villages-and-worldgen.md)):
  - **Novice (Level 1):** Bloodhound, Corgi, Great Dane, Labrador, Pug
  - **Apprentice (Level 2):** Chihuahua, Dachshund, Greyhound, Pomeranian
  - **Journeyman (Level 3):** Collie, German Shepherd, Husky, Poodle

### Stats, care, and herding

- **Health and attack:** 4 hearts when wild, 10 hearts when tamed, and 1.25 hearts of attack damage.
- **Taming and care diet:** Feed **Raw Beef** by hand or in a **Pet Bowl** to tame, feed, and breed dogs. Give them water with a Water Bucket or Pet Bowl.
- **Healing foods:** Tamed dogs (and vanilla wolves) also heal when fed most Animaniax meats, cheese wedges, omelettes, truffles, and Bottles of Milk.
- **Hunting:** Wild dogs hunt sheep and rabbits when awake. They never attack baby turtles.
- **German Shepherd herding:** A tamed, awake, standing **German Shepherd** herds nearby **sheep and goats** within 10 blocks, guiding them along its walking path!
- **Sleep:** Dogs sleep at **night** (sunset to dawn) on a **Dog Pillow** or **Straw**.
- **Breeding:** Two Content, fertile, opposite-sex adult dogs can breed and pass down a parent's breed and coat color.
