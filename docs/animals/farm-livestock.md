# Farm Livestock

Cows, mooshrooms, pigs, goats, sheep, and rabbits all gain named breeds, sex-based breeding, daily care needs, and improved drops.

## Cows and Mooshrooms

### Breeds and where to find them

| Breed | Prime beef | Milk produced | Natural biomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Angus** | Yes | Vanilla Milk Bucket | Jungles, Badlands, Swamps |
| **Friesian** | No | Friesian Milk Bucket | Plains, Meadows |
| **Hereford** | Yes | Vanilla Milk Bucket | Hills, Mountains, Peaks, Groves |
| **Highland** | Yes | Vanilla Milk Bucket | Hills, Mountains, Peaks, Groves |
| **Holstein** | No | Holstein Milk Bucket | Forests, Taigas |
| **Jersey** | Yes | Jersey Milk Bucket | Swamps |
| **Texas Longhorn** | Yes | Vanilla Milk Bucket | Savannas |
| **Mooshroom** | No | Vanilla Milk Bucket | Mushroom Fields |

Cows can also spawn naturally in Badlands, Mangrove Swamps, and mountain Peaks.

### Care, milking, and special behaviors

- **Feed:** Wheat (and compatible grain crops from supported mods). Also accept common flowers for breeding.
- **Water:** Drinks 10% of a bucket per visit to a Trough (10 drinks per bucket).
- **Milking:** Only **Content, adult, lactating females** produce milk. Females start lactating after giving birth unless changed in [Configuration](../configuration.md).
  - Right-click with an **empty Bucket** for a breed Milk Bucket (or vanilla Milk Bucket).
  - Right-click with a **Glass Bottle** for a **Bottle of Milk**.
  - Milking reduces the cow's thirst below `50`, so she must drink water again before she can be milked next time.
- **Shearing Mooshrooms:** Shearing a Mooshroom turns it into a **Friesian** cow while keeping its sex.
- **Self-defense:** Bulls attack threats if animal attacks are enabled. Adult female cows hit players back for 1 heart of damage when struck.
- **`purp` secret:** Naming a Content, lactating female cow `purp` lets you milk **Lava** instead of milk with an empty Bucket or compatible fluid container!

### Drops

- **Meat:** Angus, Hereford, Texas Longhorn, Jersey, and Highland cows drop 1 to 2 **Raw Prime Beef** (plus Looting and fed/watered bonuses). Other breeds drop regular Raw Beef. Cooked meat drops if on fire.
- **Leather:** 0 to 1 Leather, plus Looting.

## Pigs

### Breeds and where to find them

| Breed | Prime pork | Natural biomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Duroc** | Yes | Jungles |
| **Hampshire** | Yes | Mountains, Hills, Peaks, Windswept Forests |
| **Large Black** | Yes | Swamps, Dark Forests, Old Growth biomes |
| **Large White** | No | Forests, Taigas |
| **Old Spot** | Yes | Forests, Taigas |
| **Yorkshire** | No | Plains |

### Care, mud, truffles, and riding

- **Feed:** Carrots, Potatoes, Poisonous Potatoes, Beetroot, Bread, or a **Slop Bucket**. Pigs can drink slop from a Trough or eat a Slop Bucket from your hand while awake (returning an empty bucket).
- **Mud:** Pigs need to walk on Mud (Animaniax Mud or vanilla Mud) to raise their mud meter to `50` or higher and stay Content.
- **Finding Truffles:** Put a Lead on a **hungry, awake adult pig** and walk it over Grass Blocks in any **Forest** biome. The pig will dig up 1 to 2 **Truffles**. Pick them up quickly, because the pig will eat dropped Truffles on the ground!
- **Riding:** Saddled pigs require **Content** status (including water and mud) to steer with a Carrot on a Stick.

### Drops

- **Meat:** Duroc, Hampshire, Large Black, and Old Spot pigs drop 1 to 2 **Raw Prime Pork** (plus Looting and fed/watered bonuses); Large White and Yorkshire drop regular Raw Porkchop.

## Goats

### Breeds and where to find them

| Breed | Prime chevon | Special trait | Natural biomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Alpine** | Yes | Goat Milk | Mountains, Hills |
| **Angora** | No | Shearable White Wool, Goat Milk | Plains |
| **Fainting** | No | Faints when you sprint into it, Goat Milk | Plains |
| **Kiko** | Yes | Goat Milk | Mountains, Hills |
| **Kinder** | No | Goat Milk | Savannas, Badlands |
| **Nigerian Dwarf** | No | Goat Milk | Deserts |
| **Pygmy** | Yes | Goat Milk | Savannas, Badlands |

Goats can spawn naturally in Plains, Savanna, Badlands, Desert, and Mountain biomes, and can spawn on sand and red sand.

### Care, wool, milk, and herding

- **Feed:** Wheat, Apples, String, or Sticks.
- **Milking:** Content, lactating adult does give a **Goat Milk Bucket** when milked with an empty Bucket.
- **Angora wool:** Right-click an unshorn adult Angora goat with Shears for 1 to 2 White Wool. Wool regrows in about 7 minutes.
- **Fainting Goats:** Sprinting into a Fainting Goat makes it freeze and fall over for about 5 seconds.
- **Sparring and herding:** Male goats can spar with each other. Goats follow tamed **German Shepherd** dogs within 10 blocks as the dog walks.

### Drops

- **Meat:** Alpine, Kiko, and Pygmy goats drop 1 to 2 **Raw Prime Chevon** (plus Looting and care bonuses); other breeds drop **Raw Chevon**.

## Sheep

### Breeds, wool, and where to find them

| Breed | Prime mutton | Wool dropped when sheared | Dyeable | Natural biomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Dorper** | Yes | Vanilla Wool | Yes | Savannas, Plains |
| **Dorset** | Yes | **Wool (Dorset, Brown)** or vanilla Wool | White coat only | Plains, Hills, Peaks, Mountains |
| **Friesian** | No | **Wool (Friesian, Black)**, **Wool (Friesian, Brown)**, or vanilla White Wool | White coat only | Plains |
| **Jacob** | No | **Wool (Jacob)** | No | Forests |
| **Merino** | No | **Wool (Merino, White)** or **Wool (Merino, Brown)** | White coat only | Badlands, Mesas, Plains, Deserts |
| **Suffolk** | Yes | **Wool (Suffolk, Brown)** or vanilla Wool | White coat only | Badlands, Mesas, Savannas |

### Care, shearing, and milking

- **Feed:** Wheat. Also accept common flowers for breeding.
- **Shearing:** Drops 1 to 2 wool blocks (regrows in about 7 minutes). See [Food and Recipes](../food-and-recipes.md) to convert breed wool into vanilla wool, carpets, banners, and beds.
- **Milking:** Content, lactating adult ewes give a **Sheep Milk Bucket** when milked with an empty Bucket.
- **Sparring and herding:** Rams can spar with each other. Sheep follow tamed **German Shepherd** dogs within 10 blocks.

### Drops

- **Meat:** Dorper, Dorset, and Suffolk sheep drop **Raw Prime Mutton** (with care bonuses); other breeds drop regular Raw Mutton.

## Rabbits

### Breeds and where to find them

| Breed | Prime rabbit | Natural biomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chinchilla** | Yes | Savannas |
| **Cottontail** | No | Forests |
| **Dutch** | No | Plains |
| **Havana** | No | Mountains, Hills, Peaks |
| **Jack** | No | Deserts, Badlands, Beaches |
| **Lop** | No | Plains, Forests |
| **New Zealand** | Yes | Forests |
| **Rex** | Yes | Savannas |

### Care, drops, and the Killer secret

- **Feed:** Wheat, Carrots, Beetroot, or Apples.
- **Sleep:** Rabbits sleep in the late afternoon and late night on Straw or Grass Blocks.
- **Drops:** Rex, New Zealand, and Chinchilla rabbits drop **Raw Prime Rabbit**; other breeds drop regular Raw Rabbit. Fed rabbits can drop extra meat, and watered rabbits can drop extra Rabbit Hide.
- **`Killer` secret:** Naming a rabbit `Killer` gives it 25 hearts of health, 2.5 hearts of attack damage, and makes it hunt players. Renaming it back to another name restores its normal stats and behavior.
