# Poultry and Draft Horses

This page covers chickens, peafowl, and draft horses.

## Chickens

### Breeds and where to find them

| Breed | Prime chicken | Egg laid | Natural biomes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Leghorn** | No | White Egg (vanilla Egg) | Plains |
| **Orpington** | Yes | White Egg (vanilla Egg) | Jungles, Swamps |
| **Plymouth Rock** | Yes | White Egg (vanilla Egg) | Mountains, Hills, Peaks, Windswept Forests |
| **Rhode Island Red** | Yes | **Brown Egg** | Forests |
| **Wyandotte** | Yes | **Brown Egg** | Forests |

Wild chickens spawn as adult hens and often generate a small flock (roosters and chicks of the same breed) nearby.

### Care, nests, and eggs

- **Feed and water:** Chickens eat seeds and poultry feed from placed **Seed Piles**, dropped seeds on the ground, or **Animal Troughs** (1 bucket gives 20 drinks).
- **Laying eggs:** Hens lay eggs inside nearby **Chicken Nests** every ~1.5 minutes by default. Hens do not drop loose eggs on the ground unless turned on in [Configuration](../configuration.md).
- **Hatching chicks:** Eggs in a nest hatch over time if an **adult, unsterilized rooster** is within 3 blocks and fewer than 15 chickens are nearby. Chicks inherit either parent's breed.
- **Egg throwing:** Throwing white or brown eggs is off by default (can be enabled in [Configuration](../configuration.md)).
- **Shedding feathers:** Adult chickens shed Feathers about every 10 minutes.
- **Drops:** All breeds except Leghorn drop **Raw Prime Chicken**; Leghorns drop regular Raw Chicken. Fed and watered adults drop extra Feathers.

## Peafowl

Peafowl spawn in **Swamps, Mangrove Swamps, and Jungles** in groups of 1 to 3 sharing the same morph.

### Morphs and sizes

- **7 Morphs:** India Blue, White, Taupe, Purple, Peach, Opal, and Charcoal.
- **Size:** Adult peacocks (males) are larger than adult peahens (females).
- **Prime meat:** India Blue, White, Peach, and Purple peafowl drop **Raw Prime Peacock**; Taupe, Opal, and Charcoal drop **Raw Peacock**.

### Care, eggs, feathers, and defense

- **Feed and water:** Same seed diet, Seed Piles, and Trough water as chickens.
- **Peafowl Eggs:** India Blue peahens lay **Blue Peafowl Eggs**; all other morphs lay **Peafowl Eggs**. Eggs hatch in Chicken Nests when an adult, unsterilized male peafowl is within 3 blocks.
- **Morph feathers:** Adult male peafowl shed colored **Peafowl Feathers** matching their morph and drop them on death (with bonuses if fed and watered).
- **Combat and frog hunting:** Peafowl fight back for 1 heart of damage if hit, knock players back, and can hunt frogs and toads to refill their hunger.

## Draft Horses

Draft Horses are heavy, strong work horses built for pulling farm vehicles.

### Where to find them and stats

- **Natural biomes:** Plains, Sunflower Plains, Savannas, and Badlands, usually spawning in small groups of 1 to 2 with family members.
- **Step height:** Adults step smoothly up 1.2-block ledges (`1.1` for foals).

### Care, riding, and hauling

- **Feed:** Wheat, Apples, or Carrots.
- **Riding:** Draft Horses spawn already tamed. Put on a Saddle and keep the horse **Content** and awake to ride it.
- **Speed boost:** Use a **Riding Crop** while riding to give your Draft Horse a **Speed IV** burst lasting 1 to 6 seconds (uses 1 durability).
- **Hauling:** Hitch a Draft Horse to a [Pull Cart, Wagon, or Animal Tiller](../tools-and-transport.md) to haul cargo, sleep on the road, or till and plant fields.
- **Drops:** 1 to 2 **Raw Horse** (plus fed, watered, and Looting bonuses) and 0 to 1 Leather.
