# Small Pets and Amphibians

Hamsters, ferrets, hedgehogs, frogs, and toads do not breed in Animaniax, so look for them in the wild or keep them as tame companions.

## Hamsters

Hamsters spawn in **Beaches, Deserts, and Badlands** in groups of 2 to 4 and come in 9 coat colors.

### Care, taming, and interactions

- **Taming and diet:** Feed **Hamster Food**, Apples, or Wheat, Melon, Beetroot, or Pumpkin Seeds to tame a hamster.
- **Cheek food storage:** Feeding an awake hamster refills its hunger timer and stores up to 5 extra snacks (shown in chat as `Hamster food: X/5`). When hurt, a hamster eats its stored food automatically to heal half a heart at a time.
- **Water:** Right-click an awake hamster with a Water Bucket or fluid container, or keep water in a nearby **Pet Bowl** or **Animal Trough** (1 bucket gives 20 drinks).
- **Sit and check status:** Right-click an awake, tamed hamster with an empty hand (without sneaking) to make it sit or stand and view its food and water status.
- **Sleep:** Hamsters sleep during the **daytime** (dawn to sunset) on **Straw**.
- **Carry on your shoulder:** Sneak and right-click an awake, tamed hamster (when not in a ball) with an empty hand to carry it on your shoulder. Sneak and right-click a solid block to set it down.
- **Hamster Balls:** Right-click a hamster with a clear or dyed [Hamster Ball](../tools-and-transport.md) to put it inside. Right-click with an empty hand to remove the ball.
- **Hamster Wheels:** A fed, tamed hamster can run inside a [Hamster Wheel](../housing-and-production.md) to generate Redstone Flux (RF) energy!

## Ferrets

**Grey Ferrets** and **White Ferrets** spawn in **Savanna** biomes in groups of 1 to 3.

### Care and behavior

- **Taming and diet:** Feed Raw Mutton, Eggs (vanilla, Brown, or Peafowl), or raw Chicken, Rabbit, or Prime small meats to tame a ferret.
- **Nest raiding:** Hungry ferrets steal eggs from nearby **Chicken Nests** and eat food from **Animal Troughs**.
- **Hunting:** Wild ferrets hunt **baby chickens** and silverfish when awake. Tamed ferrets stop attacking chicks.
- **Sleep and carrying:** Ferrets sleep at **night** (sunset to dawn) on **Straw** or **Grass Blocks**. Owned, tamed ferrets can sit when right-clicked or ride on your shoulder when you sneak and right-click with an empty hand.

## Hedgehogs

- **Standard Hedgehog:** Spawns in **Forests** in groups of 1 to 3.
- **Albino Hedgehog:** Spawns in **Swamps and Mangrove Swamps** in groups of 1 to 2.

### Care and behavior

- **Taming and diet:** Feed Eggs, small meats, Carrots, Beetroot, or Apples to tame a hedgehog.
- **Foraging and hunting:** Hungry hedgehogs forage eggs from Chicken Nests, food from Animal Troughs, and nearby crops. They also hunt silverfish, common frogs, and toads.
- **Sleep and carrying:** Hedgehogs sleep during the **daytime** (dawn to sunset) on **Straw** or **Grass Blocks**. Owned, tamed hedgehogs can sit or ride on your shoulder just like ferrets.

## Amphibians

Animaniax adds three amphibians that avoid players, chickens, and peafowl, and ignore slimes and slimeballs:

| Species | Where to find | Special behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Common Frog** | Swamps, Mangrove Swamps, Rivers | Temperate or Warm skin; `Pepe` secret |
| **Dart Frog** | Jungles and Forests | Temperate, Warm, or Cold skin; Poison contact and arrow tipping |
| **Toad** | Swamps, Mangrove Swamps, Forests | Larger (`1.3x`) frog |

- **Dart Frog poison:** Touching a Dart Frog inflicts **Poison II** for 10 seconds. Right-click a Dart Frog with a normal **Arrow** to tip it into an **Arrow of Poison** (has a short cooldown between uses).
- **`Pepe` secret:** Naming a Common Frog `Pepe` gives it 10 hearts of health and makes it attack players, ferrets, and hedgehogs with knockback and a custom death message (`"<player> was memed to death"`). Renaming it back returns it to normal.
- **Drops:** All three amphibians drop **Raw Frog Legs** (or **Cooked Frog Legs** if killed by fire).
