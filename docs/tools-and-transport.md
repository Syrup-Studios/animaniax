# Tools and Transport

Animaniax includes care tools, pet carriers, Hamster Balls, and three animal-drawn farm vehicles.

## Tools

| Tool | Crafting | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Carving Knife** | `2` Iron Ingots (top-left, top-center) + `1` Stick (middle-left) | `100` durability. Cuts cheese wheels, prime beef, prime pork, and wheat in crafting. Also right-click adult male animals to sterilize them. |
| **Riding Crop** | `2` Leather + `1` Stick (diagonal from bottom-left to top-right) | Right-click while riding a **Draft Horse** to give it a short **Speed IV** burst (`1` to `6` seconds, `1` durability per use). |
| **Animaniax Field Guide** | `1` Book + `1` Seed + `1` Straw (shapeless) | Opens the 9-page in-game reference manual. |
| **Wheel** | `4` Sticks around `1` Iron Nugget | Crafting part for Pull Carts, Wagons, and Animal Tillers. |

## Animal-drawn vehicles

Pull Carts, Wagons, and Animal Tillers can be pulled by you on foot, by a **Draft Horse** (or any vanilla horse, donkey, or mule), or by a **Pig**.

### How to hitch and unhitch

- **With a leashed animal:** Lead a horse or pig within `3` blocks of the vehicle and right-click the vehicle with an empty hand or Lead (gives your Lead back in Survival).
- **While riding:** Ride a horse or pig right next to the vehicle and right-click the vehicle with an empty hand.
- **On foot:** Walk up to the vehicle and right-click with an empty hand to pull it yourself.
- **Unhitching:** Right-click the vehicle again with an empty hand. Vehicles also unhitch automatically if the puller moves more than `12` blocks away.

### Pull Cart

**Crafting (Shaped):**

```text
[Stick]         [Wooden Planks] [Wooden Planks]
[Wooden Planks] [Iron Ingot]    [Wooden Planks]
[Wheel]         [Empty]         [Wheel]
```

- **Add chest storage:** Right-click an empty Pull Cart with a **Chest** to install a `36`-slot (`4`-row) storage chest.
- **Open chest or ride:** Sneak and right-click to open the chest once attached. If no chest is attached, sneak and right-click with an empty hand to sit inside and ride.

### Wagon

**Crafting (Shaped):**

```text
[Wool]      [Wool]          [Wooden Planks]
[White Bed] [Wooden Planks] [Chest]
[Wheel]     [Pull Cart]     [Wheel]
```

- **Built-in storage:** Comes with a `54`-slot (`6`-row) chest (sneak and right-click to open).
- **Sleep on the road:** Right-click the upper covered top of the Wagon with an empty hand at night or during a thunderstorm to sleep inside like a bed! Moving or breaking the Wagon wakes sleeping players.
- **Driver's seat:** Right-click the front section with an empty hand to sit and ride.

### Animal Tiller

**Crafting (Shaped):**

```text
[Iron Ingot]    [Iron Ingot] [Iron Ingot]
[Wooden Planks] [Iron Ingot] [Wooden Planks]
[Wheel]         [Empty]      [Wheel]
```

- **Automated tilling and planting:** Sneak and right-click to open its `27`-slot seed box and load crop seeds. While pulled over **Grass Blocks, Dirt, or Coarse Dirt** (with open air above), the Tiller plows a **3-block-wide strip** into Farmland and automatically plants seeds from its inventory.

## Carrying small pets and Hamster Balls

- **Shoulder carry:** Sneak and right-click an awake, tamed **Hamster**, **Ferret**, or **Hedgehog** with an empty hand to lift it onto your shoulder. Sneak and right-click any solid block to set it back down.
- **Carried Pet items:** Removing a hamster from a Hamster Wheel gives you a Carried Hamster item. Sneak and right-click a solid block to place the pet back in the world.
- **Hamster Balls:**
  - **Clear Hamster Ball:** Craft `8` Glass Panes in a ring (empty center).
  - **Dyed Hamster Balls:** Craft with any of the `16` Minecraft dyes to make colored Hamster Balls.
  - Right-click an awake hamster with a Hamster Ball to put it inside; right-click with an empty hand to take the ball back off.
