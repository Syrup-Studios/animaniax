# Villages and World Generation

This page covers the Pet Seller villager, wild bee hives, and how animals spawn naturally in the world.

## Pet Seller villager

Place a **Cat Bed** (either style) near an unemployed villager to assign the **Pet Seller** profession.

The Pet Seller trades emeralds for pet spawn eggs across three tiers (**Novice**, **Apprentice**, and **Journeyman**). Puppies and kittens cost **1.5x** the adult price range.

### Trade list

| Tier | Cats (adult emerald price) | Dogs (adult emerald price) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Novice (Level 1)** | American Shorthair (`10` to `20`) | Bloodhound (`15` to `30`), Corgi (`15` to `25`), Great Dane (`20` to `30`), Labrador (`20` to `30`), Pug (`15` to `25`) |
| **Apprentice (Level 2)** | Asiatic (`15` to `25`), Ragdoll (`10` to `20`), Tabby (`15` to `25`) | Chihuahua (`20` to `30`), Dachshund (`10` to `20`), Greyhound (`20` to `30`), Pomeranian (`15` to `25`) |
| **Journeyman (Level 3)** | Exotic (`15` to `25`), Norwegian (`10` to `20`), Siamese (`25` to `35`) | Collie (`20` to `30`), German Shepherd (`20` to `30`), Husky (`20` to `25`), Poodle (`15` to `25`) |

Each adult breed has separate male and female offers, while puppies and kittens choose a random sex when hatched.

## Wild Bee Hives

- **Where to find:** Wild Bee Hives generate on tree logs in **Forests, Jungles, Taigas, Swamps, and Plains** (at most one per chunk).
- **Watch out for stings:** Wild hives periodically sting nearby players for 1.25 hearts. Mine the hive and surround it with 8 Wooden Planks to craft a peaceful **Bee Hive** that produces honey faster.

## Wild herds, flocks, and natural spawning

- **Livestock families:** Naturally spawned cows, mooshrooms, pigs, sheep, goats, rabbits, and draft horses often generate with an opposite-sex partner or baby nearby.
- **Chicken flocks:** Wild chickens spawn as adult hens and often generate a small flock of roosters or chicks of the same breed.
- **Wolf and ocelot replacement:** By default, Animaniax Dogs and Cats replace natural vanilla Wolf and Ocelot spawns in their matching biomes.
- **Population caps:** To prevent lag and overcrowding, natural spawning pauses in an area once nearby counts reach the default group cap (`40` per farm or small-pet group, or `20` for cats and dogs). You can adjust spawn limits in [Configuration](configuration.md).
